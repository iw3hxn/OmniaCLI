Machines

A clean Ubuntu (22.04, 24.04) or Debian (11, 12, 13) server, prepared from here: its updates, nginx, certbot, Docker, OmniaCLI with the workspaces, a firewall, then linked to this OmniaCLI through SSH. Its workspaces and instances are then made from its pages, opened here through the link. About 10 to 20 minutes.

1. Let OmniaCLI in

{% if provision_key %}

On the new machine, as root, add OmniaCLI's provisioning key to root's keys (or to a user with sudo without a password):

mkdir -p ~/.ssh && chmod 700 ~/.ssh && echo '{{ provision_key }}' >> ~/.ssh/authorized_keys

This key prepares machines only; the links use another one, which may only forward to OmniaCLI. Take it out of authorized_keys once the machine is ready if you want: the link does not need it.
